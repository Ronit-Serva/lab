## A problem close to home

Seeing a family member struggle with managing finances made me want to make financial organization more intuitive. It was a concrete problem I could turn into a design and software project.

## What I built

I designed a personal finance management framework and built a web application around it. The goal was to make everyday financial organization easier to understand and use.

The application is now available at [serva-aica.web.app](https://serva-aica.web.app/). Open it to explore the current experience.

## The questions that shaped it

How can an interface make financial information clearer? What should it explain rather than hide? How can software help someone understand their finances while keeping them in control?

Those questions connect this practical build to my broader interest in financial systems and useful software.

## What comes next

The live application is the starting point for a fuller case study. I want to document its workflows, show the interface, and explain the design decisions and lessons from building it. Detailed testing results and measured user outcomes are not yet included here.
